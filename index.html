Our Story

A table set forevery craving.

Garnish is a cafe and restaurant in Hayathnagar built around one simple idea — that good food deserves a beautiful setting. Our kitchen moves comfortably between worlds: creamy continental pastas and loaded pizzas, wok-tossed Chinese favourites, slow-sealed dum biryani and indulgent shakes.

Everything we serve comes from a hygienic kitchen with fresh ingredients and real attention to flavour. Whether it is a quick burger, a long family lunch or a late dessert with friends, the promise stays the same: bold flavours, a premium experience, and a reason to come back.
